A customer may return an item for a refund within a fixed window, provided the
item is unused and they can show a receipt. Faulty goods are handled
differently: the window does not apply to them.

## Returning an item

- An item may be returned within **30 days**.[^owner-interview-2026-09-13]
- The customer must present a **receipt**.[^owner-interview-2026-09-13]
- The item must be **unused**.[^owner-interview-2026-09-13]

## When the refund arrives

Once a return is accepted, the refund lands within **5 working
days**.[^owner-interview-2026-09-13]

Open question: does the 5 working days count from the day the return is
accepted, or from the day the item is received back?

## Faulty goods

Faulty goods are **exempt from the 30-day window** — a customer may return a
faulty item after the window has passed.[^owner-interview-2026-09-13]
